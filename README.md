# Hill's Gadget Sale Analysis
The dataset contained approximately 301 rows of sales records relating to Hill's Gadget Company's business activities over the two-year period.


### Introduction

This report presents an analysis of sales performance at Hill's Gadget Company over a two-year period, covering 2023 and 2024. The objective of the analysis was to evaluate the company's sales performance, identify year-on-year changes in revenue and costs, examine regional sales distribution, assess product performance, and identify monthly sales trends.

### Methodology
Data preparation and analysis were conducted using Microsoft Excel.

- Converted the dataset into an Excel Table.
- Created additional columns to extract the year and month.
- Used Pivot Tables to summarise and compare sales performance.
- Conducted year-on-year (YoY) analysis to evaluate changes in sales and costs.
- Analysed regional sales, product performance, and monthly trends.

#### Overall Sales Performance

- Total quantity went from 724 in 2023 and increased to 899 in 2024. Which is 24% increase in demand.
- Sales increased from $365.6K in 2023 to $475.6K in 2024, representing approximately 30.1% growth.

#### Cost and Profit Performance

- Costs increased from $57.5K to $64K, representing approximately 11% growth.
- Profit increased by approximately 34%, going from 308.1k in 2023 to 411.6k in 2024
  
  Regional Performance

- The South consistently recorded the highest sales across both years.
- The East experienced significant sales growth in 2024 despite being the lowest-performing region in 2023.

#### Product Performance

- The six product categories analysed were headphones, keyboards, laptops, monitors, mice, and printers.
- Keyboards generated the highest sales in 2023, while monitors took the lead in 2024, followed by mice.

Monthly Sales Trends

-May recorded relatively low sales across the two-year period.

- February was a peak sales period in 2023, while October recorded stronger sales in 2024.

Recommendations
- Target Low-Sales Periods: Investigate the reasons for lower sales in May and - introduce targeted promotions where appropriate. Review December's performance to determine whether additional festive campaigns are needed.
- Introduce Product Bundling: Pair popular products, such as monitors, with lower-performing complementary products, such as keyboards or printers, to encourage additional purchases and increase average transaction value.

 ### Conclusion

Hill's Gadget Company achieved strong sales growth in 2024, with revenue increasing by approximately 30.1% compared with 2023. The analysis identified opportunities to improve sales during weaker periods, strengthen regional performance, and increase sales across product categories. Implementing targeted strategies and monitoring their results could help the company sustain growth and make more informed business decisions.
